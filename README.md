# Automated Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful bussiness insights through data cleaning, data analysis, PivotTables, and an interactive dashboard.

The project follows a structured workflow from raw data preparation to dashboard reporting. It also demonstrates how Excel reporting can be refreshed when the underlying data is updated.

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, profit, orders, and quantity.
* Compare yearly , monthly , weekly sales performance.
* Evaluate regional and state-wise business performance.
* Understand order status and product return patterns.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting.
* Reduce repetitive manual reporting through Power Query and PivotTable refreshes.

## Tools and Skills Used

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, PivotTables, charts, KPI cards, and dashboard design.
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation of analysis-ready data.
* **PivotTables:** Business performance analysis and summary reporting.
* **PivotCharts:** Monthly trends, weekly trends, and category comparisons.
* **Slicers and Report Connections:** Interactive filtering across connected dashboard components.
* **Excel Number Formatting:** Currency, percentages, and KPI display formats.

## Project Workflow

1. **Raw Data:** Started with the original sales dataset containing inconsistent and messy data.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Used Power Query to clean and standardize the data, including city names, null values, inconsistent text, and other data-quality issues.
3. **PivotTable Analysis:** Created PivotTables to analyze sales, profit, order volume, profitability, customer segments, regions, states, and returns.
4. **Business Insights:** Documented key findings from the analysis in a separate worksheet.
5. **Interactive Dashboard:** Created KPI cards, charts, slicers, and Report Connections to explore business performance.
6. **Refresh-Based Reporting:** Designed the workflow so that refreshed source data can flow through the Power Query and PivotTable reporting process.


## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Sales:** ₹108.55 crore
* **Total Profit:** ₹30.04 crore
* **Total Orders:** 12,000
* **Total Quantity:** 55,773
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** Approximately ₹90,455
* **Return Rate:** Displayed on the dashboard using returned orders as a percentage of total orders.

*Note: Figures are based on the current workbook. AOV is calculated as Total Sales divided by Total Orders.*

## Key Business Insights

1. **Year-wise Sales Performance:** Sales in 2025 were approximately 1.92% lower than in 2024, indicating a decline in annual sales performance.
2. **Monthly Sales Performance:** August recorded the highest monthly sales, while February recorded the lowest monthly sales in the analysis.
3. **Regional Profitability:** The West region recorded the highest profit margin among the four regions, at approximately 28.04%.
4. **Revenue Contribution by States:** The analysis evaluates the combined revenue contribution of the top five states to understand geographical revenue concentration.
5. **City-wise Discount Analysis:** Average discounts were compared with sales and profit across cities to investigate the relationship between discounting and profitability.
6. **Product Profitability:** Product-level analysis identifies the contribution of top-performing products to overall profit.
7. **Customer Segment Analysis:** Customer segments were compared by revenue contribution, profit, and order value to understand their business importance.
8. **Return Analysis:** Returned orders were analyzed against total orders to measure the overall return rate and identify patterns across product categories and order statuses.


## Automation and Reporting Approach

Power Query prepares the data for analysis, while PivotTables summarize the cleaned dataset. The dashboard uses these summaries to display KPIs and visualizations.

When new data is added to the configured source, the reporting workflow can be updated using **Refresh All**, provided the new data is included in the source and the queries and PivotTables are configured correctly.

This approach helps reduce repetitive manual work and supports more consistent sales reporting.
